Crowd

Ten club-goers, three states each. wait bobs on the beat, good jumps up on a hit, bad on a miss. Five are dealt per round and stand under the booth behind a frosted band; the whole crowd rocks left–right with the beat and reacts together at the end.

Drawn in code

Club lightsA kick-synced pulse on the background, two sweeping beams in the accent colours, and a white flash on every hit.
Frosted bandA blurred, semi-transparent strip behind the crowd so they read against the camera feed without a full background.
Platter ringsJudge rings on the two platters with an approach disc per note and a pulse ring after each hit, the same system as Wink Wink.
ResultConfetti on a good round, a big percentage, a verdict line, the crowd’s final reaction, and the face stickers above.

Sound

All synthesized with WebAudio except the ending stinger.

Groove80 bpm, 15-second set. Kick, snare, hats, bass and a chord loop (C, Dm, Em, Dm). Scheduled ahead so it stays locked to the chart.
Hit soundsEach hit plays the next note of a 12-note riff (C5 up to C6 and back), so a clean run sounds like a melody. Misses scratch the record and draw a low boo.
CrowdCheer on a good hit, groan on a miss.
Stingerending-great.mp3 on 50% or better, shared with the salon and jump-rope games. Below that, the record scratch and a boo.

Rules & input

Two inputs, one chart.

Round15 seconds at 80 bpm. Notes fall onto the left and right platters; hit them on the beat. Every eighth beat is a drop that wants both at once.
Blink DJLeft eye for the left platter, right eye for the right, both eyes for the drop. Same eye tracking as Wink Wink: blendshape difference over 0.18, 70 ms single-vs-both window, 40 ms lag compensation. Perfect ±280 ms, Good ±600 ms.
Nod the BeatTilt your head left or right for the platters, nod for the drop. Roll is the angle of the eye line, pitch is the nose against the eye midpoint. A tilt of about 6° triggers and re-arms once you are back within 3°; the resting pose is calibrated over the first 30 frames and drifts slowly. Perfect ±320 ms, Good ±700 ms.
ChartStrict left–right alternation on every beat, drop every eighth beat. First note lands about 2.7 s after GO.
Safe zones390 × 694 canvas. Platters and HUD inside the core zone (x 65–325, y 82–533); the crowd below the booth stays inside the visual zone. ?zones shows the overlay, ?debug logs detection.
FlowCamera card → "Blink to start" (blink) or three-second warm-up (head) → count-in → 15 s → result with face stickers.
